## 2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information when you:

- visit the OpenStore website or web app;
- create or use an OpenStore account;
- browse app listings, collections, charts, reviews, and editorial content;
- use library, billing, device, notification, review, or report features;
- contact support or send legal, privacy, or operational requests.

This policy applies to the features we actually operate. Product-roadmap ideas described elsewhere do not automatically mean those features are live.

## 3. Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you use OpenStore, we may collect:

- Account and profile data, such as name, email address, region, and account identifiers.
- Device and service data, such as device name, platform, trusted-device status, and last-seen information.
- Library and transaction data, such as installed or queued items, wishlist status, purchase-history records, restore actions, and billing-related records.
- User content, such as ratings, review titles, review text, report reasons, report details, and support messages.
- Technical and usage data, such as logs needed to keep the service available, secure, and functioning.
- Compliance and legal data, such as communications about abuse, fraud, rights requests, complaints, disputes, or platform-safety issues.

As of the current repository state on 2026-03-21, OpenStore does not implement live third-party advertising technology or third-party marketing analytics in the repository codebase. If that changes, we will update this policy and obtain consent where required by law.

## 4. Sources of Personal Information

We may collect personal information:

- directly from you;
- from your browser, device, or app session;
- from your actions inside OpenStore, such as posting a review or filing a report;
- from developers, support partners, payment providers, or other service providers where needed to complete a transaction or investigate an issue;
- from legal or regulatory authorities when required.

## 5. How We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information to:

- provide, maintain, and secure OpenStore;
- create and manage accounts;
- show library, billing, device, and notification features;
- process reviews, reports, moderation, and safety operations;
- communicate with you about support, policy, transactions, security, or service changes;
- detect abuse, fraud, and technical problems;
- comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms;
- improve product quality using service metrics, debugging, and aggregated analysis.

Where a jurisdiction requires a legal basis, we rely on one or more of the following as applicable:

- performance of a contract;
- your consent;
- our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving OpenStore;
- compliance with legal obligations;
- protection of vital interests, public-interest tasks, or other bases recognized by applicable law.

## 6. When We Share Personal Information

We may disclose personal information:

- to service providers that host, secure, support, or operate OpenStore for us;
- to payment, billing, fraud-prevention, and customer-support partners if live commercial features are enabled;
- to developers or content partners where needed to handle app-specific issues, review moderation, refund requests, or abuse reports;
- to legal, regulatory, law-enforcement, or judicial authorities when required or when necessary to protect rights, safety, or the service;
- to buyers, investors, or transaction counterparties as part of a merger, restructuring, financing, or sale, subject to appropriate confidentiality steps.

Reviews and similar public submissions may be visible to other users. Abuse reports and support submissions are generally not public, but we may share the substance of a report with the relevant developer, partner, or authority when reasonably necessary to investigate or resolve the issue.

We do not describe ourselves as a data broker and do not sell personal information in the ordinary sense of a standalone data-broker business model.

## 7. International Transfers

OpenStore may use service providers and infrastructure in more than one country. This means personal information may be transferred, stored, or accessed outside your home jurisdiction.

Where applicable law requires additional steps for cross-border transfers, we will use the safeguards required by that law, such as contractual protections, adequacy mechanisms, localization measures, or other recognized transfer tools.

## 8. Data Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including:

- while your account is active;
- while we provide a requested service or transaction;
- for a reasonable period to handle refunds, disputes, support, moderation, and security issues;
- as needed to comply with legal, tax, accounting, audit, or recordkeeping obligations.

Different categories may have different retention periods. We may also retain de-identified, aggregated, or incident-response records for longer where allowed by law.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

- access personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate data;
- request deletion, anonymization, or restriction;
- object to certain processing;
- withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
- request portability where available;
- complain to a privacy or data-protection regulator.

To exercise a rights request, contact `[Privacy Contact Email]`. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity and protect other users.

## 10. Children and Age-Sensitive Features

OpenStore is not intended for children below the minimum age permitted by local law to use the service without any required parent or guardian authorization. If we learn that we collected personal information in a way that violates applicable child-data rules, we will take reasonable steps to delete or limit that data.

## 11. Security

We use reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Complaints, Contact, and Changes

If you have a privacy question, complaint, or rights request, contact:

- `[Privacy Contact Email]`
- `[Privacy Contact Address]`

If we cannot resolve your concern, you may have the right to contact the privacy or data-protection authority in your jurisdiction.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a revised effective date and, where required by law, provide additional notice.
