# OpenStore Privacy Policy and Terms Pack

This directory contains draft privacy-policy and terms-of-service materials for OpenStore. These documents are drafting tools, not legal advice. Replace all bracketed placeholders before publication and have local counsel review any jurisdiction that matters to launch.

## What This Pack Includes

- `policy-intake.yaml`
  - Source-of-truth placeholders for company identity, privacy contact, governing law, and launch posture.
- `privacy-policy.en.md`
  - English working source for the privacy policy.
- `terms-of-service.en.md`
  - English working source for the terms of service.
- `privacy-policy.translations.md`
  - Working translations of the privacy policy.
- `terms-of-service.translations.md`
  - Working translations of the terms of service.

## Why the EU Is Handled This Way

The earlier legal task was framed by jurisdictions rather than languages. Because the EU does not map to a single language, this policy pack uses:

- English as the general global source
- French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, and Portuguese as working EU-language drafts

If a full EU-24 localization set is needed later, extend it from the English source after the company entity, controller details, and governing-law approach are finalized.

## Current Repository Assumptions Locked Into These Drafts

As of 2026-03-21, the repository supports the following safe assumptions:

- OpenStore is currently a web-first storefront prototype.
- The repository includes account, library, device, billing, review, and report concepts.
- The Prisma schema includes personal data fields such as email, name, region, device information, review content, and library history.
- The current repository does not show live advertising tech or third-party marketing analytics.
- The policies therefore describe the current product posture conservatively and tell the reader that future live features may add more detail.

## Required Publication Inputs

Before publishing, fill:

- Company legal name
- Registered address
- Privacy contact email
- Support contact
- Effective date
- Governing law and dispute forum
- Child age threshold, if a market-specific threshold is used
- Payment provider details, if live billing is enabled
- Hosting or transfer regions, if they are known and material
